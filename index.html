Quickstart

One endpoint. Fresh account data.

A request without payment returns the x402 payment challenge. Use an x402-compatible client to pay and retry.

curl -i "https://aave-health-api.onrender.com/api/v1/aave/health?wallet=0x...&chain=base"

Replace 0x... with the complete wallet address. Valid chains: arbitrum, base, polygon.
